Solo project · Game Design 3

Mirror Hop

Jump the platforms, jump the mirrors. A 3D platformer where you use mirrors and your own reflection to get through the level.

A solo project for the Game Design 3 course at Howest DAE in 2025, made in Unity.

Watch the trailer

Each level is split over two worlds: the normal one and the mirror world. Platforms, paths and enemies exist in only one of them, so the full route only appears when you switch between both.

Three ways into the mirror world

  • Enter a mirror. Static mirrors work as gates that put you in the other world.
  • Swap with your reflection. Interact with your reflection in a mirror and you trade places with it.
  • Launch a clone. Hold to aim, release to send a mirror clone forward, then swap with it to land on a platform in the other world. While you aim, time slows down.

Movement

  • Double jump, with a change of direction after the first jump.
  • One dash, only in the air.
  • A wall jump that pushes you away from the wall, so you can't climb the same wall.

What I did

All the design, the mechanics prototyping and programming, and some of the character animation. The level art in the final build comes from an asset store pack.

Client project · Applied Games

Untitled Survey Game

A survey has never been this magical. You play a tiny frog wizard on an office desk, and every survey question is a small minigame.

Made for the client Change Gamer in the Applied Games course (2025), together with a team of 4 students. Built in Unity for the browser.

Watch the trailer

Change Gamer makes sustainability (ESG) plans for companies based on what their employees think. They asked for a game to replace a boring survey: employees play it for about 5 minutes a day, for 5 days.

The players are office workers who often never play games, on a work PC in the browser. So everything is controlled with the mouse, there is very little UI, and each minigame has a short tutorial.

What I did

Minigame and level design. I also modeled and animated the frog wizard and the small frogs.

Play in browser

One minigame per question type

Each type of survey question got its own level, all on the same desk.

The frogs

I modeled and animated the frog wizard you play as, and the small frogs you sort in the ranking level.

The small frogs

In the ranking level, each answer is a small frog. You pick one up and put it on a step of the staircase to rank it.

Game jam · Xbox Game Camp Belgium

Monster Mines

Clean up a bunker full of dangerous mines and poisonous gas. Sounds easy, right? But you're not alone down there.

Made in 3 days at Xbox Game Camp Belgium 2026 with a team of 5, for the Minesweeper theme set by Xbox.

Game design: figuring out the whole game with the team, including what the player actually does with the mines.

3D art and animation: I modeled, textured, rigged and animated the monster, and made the mines.

Unity: I put it all together in Unity and triggered the monster's animations from code.
